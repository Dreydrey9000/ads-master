# Vision

> "The epicenter of ads: research on what's working, what ads you have that are doing well, slots for all platforms, insights on how they're doing from AI's perspective and what to change — the good, the bad, the ugly — what ads are crushing right now, what people are spending money on, what to change, what to do about it, how it affects me, and multiple businesses on my account." — Drey

## Pillars
1. **Unified performance** — every platform's spend, ROAS, CPA, CTR side by side, per business.
2. **AI verdicts** — Good / Bad / Ugly, each with a concrete action ("move 20% of LinkedIn budget to ChatGPT Ads").
3. **Market research** — live winning ads from ad libraries (Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center, Foreplay).
4. **Consumer spend intelligence** — category spending trends mapped to each business as tailwinds or headwinds. *Nobody else does this.*
5. **Multi-business** — agencies and multi-brand founders run everything from one login.
